## The 'Why?'

Though my expenses are fairly constant over the last few months, I have been lending money or payments via credit card to my friends and family. 
Though most of the money is recovered, there would be sudden surprises in the credit card bills or huge deviation in account balances to the mental figure in my mind. Having multiple accounts made it difficult to view all the outflows and inflows. For this, I needed a unified dashboard.
