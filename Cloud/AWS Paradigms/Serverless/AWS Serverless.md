## What is Serverless?

Serverless ***DOES NOT*** mean there are no servers in the backend performing a job or simply running a code, what the term serverless actually means is that you don't have to provision and manage the infrastructure, as you can offload all of this to AWS. AWS abstracts all these activities allowing us to focus on the core development

>[!Note]
>Not all services and offerings from AWS are serverless, you might need to choose the right service based on the requirement

Some of the activities which AWS abstracts are:
- Choosing and patching your OS.
- Installing the required runtimes.
- Configuring auto scaling and load balancing.
- Maintaining High availability.

Some of the activities the customer is responsible for are:
- Building and running your code.
- Monitoring your applications.
